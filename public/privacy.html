Privacy Policy

My Finance Manager, by Patni AI Lab · Last updated 23 September 2026

My Finance Manager is a personal budgeting tool. It is built to keep your financial records on your own device by default. This policy explains exactly what is stored, what leaves your device, and what never does.

1. What the app stores on your device

Everything you enter — income, expenses, budgets, savings goals, debts, net worth entries and monthly review notes — is saved in your browser's local storage on the device you are using. This data is not transmitted anywhere unless you choose to sign in (see section 2).

2. Signing in with Google (optional)

The app works fully without an account. If you choose to sign in with Google in order to sync across devices, we use Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. In that case:

DataWhy
Your Google account name, email address, profile photo and account IDTo identify your account and show who is signed in
Your financial records listed in section 1To sync them between your devices

Synced records are stored under your own user ID and are readable and writable only by your authenticated account. Security rules on the database deny all cross-account access.

3. The AI Coach

If you ask the AI Coach a question, a summary of your financial figures for the selected month (totals, category amounts, budget percentages, emergency fund, and each debt's balance, interest rate, minimum payment and the creditor label you typed for it) is sent to Google's Gemini API to generate the answer, together with your question. Your name, email address and account ID are not included — but avoid putting personal details in creditor labels if you would rather they were not sent. If no AI key is configured, the app answers from a built-in rule engine instead and nothing is sent anywhere.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties.
  • We do not show advertising and do not use advertising identifiers.
  • We do not run third-party analytics or tracking scripts in the app.
  • We do not ask for, receive or store bank credentials, card numbers or account numbers. The app has no connection to any bank and cannot move money.

5. Deleting your data

To remove local data, use your browser or device settings to clear site data for this app, or reinstall it. To remove synced data, sign in and use "Start fresh" from the tour, or email the address below and we will delete the records held under your account. You may also revoke the app's access from your Google account permissions.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

7. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top will be updated and the revised policy will be published at this address.
